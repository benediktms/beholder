## Why

Agents need a reproducible structural view of a revision-consistent semantic graph, but clusters must never be mistaken for canonical facts or silently block semantic reads. This change establishes a derived, evidence-preserving topology and empirically gates a local-first Leiden CPM backend before it becomes useful output.

## What Changes

- Add algorithm-neutral topology projection, complete derived identities, deterministic manifests, and flat Leiden CPM partitions.
- Preserve all directed canonical evidence while recording explicit participant, layer, filtering, weighting, self-edge, and parallel-edge rules.
- Persist derived artifacts/readiness separately from Mnestic and run them through a non-blocking, coalesced durable topology job.
- Expose independent coarse/standard/fine profiles without hierarchical claims, only after audited fixture and utility gates.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `topology-analysis`: Deterministic, rebuildable derived topology and partition contracts.

### Modified Capabilities
- `indexing-pipeline`: Observable derived-job lifecycle, readiness, currentness, and bounded retention behavior.

## Impact

Implementation is confined to proposed topology-analysis and derived-state modules plus narrow daemon/job/protocol integration. Canonical Mnestic facts, enrichment payloads, and publication remain untouched; Infomap and SBM stay separate experiments.
